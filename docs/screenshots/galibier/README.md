# Col du Galibier rendering

The live 3D world behind the ride HUD for [Increase Your Threshold Power](http://localhost:4200/classes/increase-your-threshold-power?simulate), which rides the pass road. The world follows its description: a high alpine road climbing past snow banks to the Tour monument. It is built on the shared street kit (`world-kit.ts`) on the pattern of Old Town Criterium: one module, `world-galibier.ts`, with `build`, `lay`, `place`, `setTime` and `sink`, seeded detail, canvas textures, the kit's `Figures` for the fans, and a loop that recycles the pass road. Each capture is the same opening frame of the ride at 1440 × 900 on Three.js's WebGL2 backend, and each iteration is merged in its own pull request.

Fans in team jerseys stand along the verge waving flags, and the black slate scree climbs on the right. On the left the slope falls to the valley, and across it La Meije's glaciated peaks stand under a clear sky. In the first take the snow banks were stretched, striped boxes and the peaks were buried under oversized flickering snow caps. The banks are now rounded heaps, the caps sit clear of the rock above a dark band, and the range is spread back across the valley. | 80%            |

## Reproducing a capture

Build the app (`npm run build` in `frontend/`), serve `dist/wattzup/browser` on port 4300 with `node scripts/serve-dist.mjs 4300`, open `/classes/increase-your-threshold-power?simulate`, click **Ride in Col du Galibier**, wait for `/world`, let the scene settle for eight seconds and capture the viewport at 1440 × 900.
