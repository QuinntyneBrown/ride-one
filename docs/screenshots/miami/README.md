# Miami Neon Nights rendering iterations

The live 3D world behind the ride HUD for [Ride to the Beat](http://localhost:4200/classes/ride-to-the-beat?simulate), whose class rides Miami Neon Nights (the neon boulevard). The composition reference is [the Miami mock](../../mocks/pages/world-ride/miami.html): a beachfront boulevard on a wet Miami night, neon-traced art deco hotels, palms and convertibles on the right, the moonlit Atlantic on the left and the bay's towers ahead. The level of detail aimed for is Old Town's ([its iterations](../oldtown/README.md)). Each iteration is one significant change, captured from the same opening frame of the ride at 1440 × 900 on Three.js's WebGL2 backend, and merged in its own pull request.

| Capture                                                | Change and observed result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [000 · Initial](000-initial.png)                       | Flat pastel boxes with a grid of dark windows, neon lines along the rooflines, a blade sign on each hotel, palms on both sidewalks, a row of towers across the bay and a grey disc for a moon.                                                                                                                                                     |
| [001 · Deco façades](001-deco-facades.jpg)             | The hotel fronts are drawn at twice the resolution as art deco stucco: fluted pilasters between the bays, a lit string course and shadow under each storey, racing stripes at the corners, and paired windows with mullions, transoms and a shadowed eyebrow over each. Lit windows glow warm, pink or blue, and some have their blinds half down. |
| [002 · Deco crowns](002-deco-crowns.jpg)               | Each hotel's roofline rises to a deco crown: a stepped ziggurat of three tiers or a two-tier tower of fins, every tier's edge traced in the hotel's neon and a lit mast and finial on top. The stepped silhouettes read down the street against the violet sky.                                                                                    |
| [003 · Moon](003-moon.jpg)                             | The grey disc becomes a full moon with darker seas and bright craters, wrapped in a soft cool halo drawn additively in the humid air, so it lights the sky over the sea.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| [004 · Stars and clouds](004-stars-clouds.jpg)         | A hazy city's handful of stars sprinkles the sky above the glow, a few bright enough to show a cross, and thin bands of cloud drift past the moon, violet underneath where the city lights them.                                                                                                                                                   |
| [005 · Wet reflections](005-wet-reflections.jpg)       | Each hotel's neon is mirrored in the wet street: long, broken streaks of its colour run towards the rider across the sidewalk under the blade sign and down the near lanes, drawn additively and fading with distance from the light. The road finally reads as wet.                                                                               |
| [006 · Deco lamps](006-deco-lamps.jpg)                 | Deco lamp posts stand every 24 m along the kerb and the promenade wall, staggered between sides: a tapered white column on a round base, a ringed collar and a frosted globe glowing warm, with a halo round it in the damp air and a soft pool of light on the ground. The rhythm of globes leads the eye down the boulevard.                     |
| [007 · Road markings](007-road-markings.jpg)           | The road is redrawn across its full width over 16 m: pale aggregate, tar seams and cracks, darker puddles smeared with neon, a white edge line inside each gutter, a worn double yellow centre line and a cast-iron manhole cover in the right lane. The boulevard reads as a real two-lane street.                                                |
| [008 · Lobbies and marquees](008-lobbies-marquees.jpg) | Each hotel's ground floor opens as a lit lobby behind plate glass: warm walls under chandeliers, a reception desk, guests and potted palms on a terrazzo floor between dark glazing bars. Over the door a marquee canopy projects 3 m, its fascia ringed with two rows of bulbs and its underside glowing in the hotel's neon.                     |
| [009 · Sidewalk cafés](009-sidewalk-cafes.jpg)         | Each hotel sets out a sidewalk café between its marquee and the palms: round white tables on pedestal bases, four chairs drawn up to each, a candle glowing in a small halo, and an umbrella over every table in white, pink, teal or lemon. The right-hand sidewalk now reads as Ocean Drive's dining strip.                                      |
| [010 · Convertibles](010-convertibles.jpg)             | The parked cars become classic convertibles ([detail](010-convertibles-detail.jpg)): a long candy-painted body with a lower bonnet and boot, tail fins and chrome side spears, a cream bench seat in the open tub, a chrome-framed windscreen, white-wall tyres on chrome hubs, glowing tail lights and a pool of neon underglow on the wet road.  |
| [011 · Oncoming traffic](011-oncoming-traffic.jpg)     | Five convertibles cruise the far lane towards the rider at 8 to 11 m/s on the ride's clock, headlights on: a flare at each lamp and two warm beams spreading 16 m ahead of them on the wet road. They start 45 m or more ahead, so none sits on the camera at the start. The boulevard is no longer parked-up and still.                           |
| [012 · Lifeguard towers](012-lifeguard-towers.jpg)     | South Beach's painted lifeguard towers stand on the sand every 80 m, 12 to 17 m beyond the wall, closed for the night with their ramps towards the promenade: pastel huts on white stilts under pyramid roofs, a neon band round each deck and a lamp glowing by the hut. They break up the long run of sea on the left.                           |
| [013 · Skyline](013-skyline.jpg)                       | The bay's skyline is rebuilt in two ranks: a nearer rank of 24 towers stepping back in up to three setbacks, some with lit edges up their corners, neon crowns and spires with blinking red aircraft beacons, and a lower rank of 30 behind filling the gaps. The towers read as a city rather than a row of boxes.                                |

## Reproducing a capture

Start the app with `npm start` in `frontend/`. Open `/classes/ride-to-the-beat?simulate`, click **Ride in Miami Neon Nights**, wait for `/world`, let the scene settle for six seconds and capture the viewport at 1440 × 900.
