# Neon Docklands rendering iterations

The live 3D world behind the ride HUD for [20 Minute Power Intervals](http://localhost:4200/classes/20-minute-power-intervals?simulate), whose class rides Neon Docklands (Harbour Bridges Loop). The composition reference is [the Docklands mock still](../../mocks/assets/world-ride/docklands.webp): a night street between lit towers with neon signs, a harbour skyline to the left, lamps, a raised quay pavement and neon kerb lines. Each iteration is one significant change, captured from the same opening frame of the ride at 1440 × 900 on Three.js's WebGL2 backend, and merged in its own pull request.

The far end of the street still opens onto an empty black sky: a harbour skyline is next.                                               |

The street below still lacks its quay pavements, lamps and neon kerb lines.                                                                    |

| [005 · Lamps](005-lamps.jpg)             | Street lamps stand on the quay as in the mock: slim dark poles with a glowing globe 6 m up, every 40 m on alternate sides. The towers' bases still meet the pavement without shopfronts or doors.                                                                                                                        |
| [006 · Lobbies](006-lobbies.jpg)         | Each street tower now has a street floor: 4.6 m of glazing towards the road, lit warm or cool from inside with some bays dark, under a dark steel canopy. The towers now meet the pavement as buildings rather than boxes, and their light warms the quay. The skyline's towers and the street's upper floors still glow evenly: the neon signs could carry more of the colour.                                                                    |
| [007 · Blade signs](007-blade-signs.jpg) | The thin neon strips are replaced by blade signs: 2 m panels on steel brackets standing out from the façades over the canopies, 6 to 16 m tall, with words such as OPEN, CAFE, HOTEL and RAMEN in glowing tube letters, each word in its own neon colour. They face down the street, so the rider reads them as they come up. The tower layout is unchanged. The road surface is still a plain dark grey with no reflections of the city's lights. |

## Reproducing a capture

Start the app with `npm start` in `frontend/`. Open the class page with `?simulate`, click **Ride in Neon Docklands**, wait for `/world`, let the scene settle for six seconds and capture the viewport at 1440 × 900.
