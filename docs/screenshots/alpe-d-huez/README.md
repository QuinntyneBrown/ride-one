# Alpe d'Huez

## Bends and elevation

Fixes to how the road bends and climbs here (L2-101 AC6, L2-110 AC9), from the series that began with Old Town.

| Capture | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| [021 · The fans' paint lies on the climb](bend-021-paint-on-the-hairpins.jpg) | The names and cries painted across the hairpins were flat 8 and 10 m plates laid level, so on the Alpe's 8.5% most of each word sank into the tarmac and only the end nearest the rider showed. They now lie along the slope, and the roadside props behind the rider take the road's height there instead of the height under the rider. |
