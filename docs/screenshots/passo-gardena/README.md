# Passo Gardena

## Bends and elevation

Fixes to how the road bends and climbs here (L2-101 AC6, L2-110 AC9), from the series that began with Old Town.

| --- | --- |
| [023 · The painted rows lie on the pass](bend-023-paint-on-the-pass.jpg) | The painted names and slogans on the road up from Selva were laid level, so on the pass's 6% the big SELLARONDA across the road sank into the tarmac. | [045 · The Cir spires stand over the pass](bend-045-cir-spires-stand.jpg) | The Cir spires and the Sella's walls over the pass were bent vertex by vertex with the road's 55° bends, slewing and squeezing the towers of rock with each turn up from Selva. They now stand beyond the road unbent, as the panorama does, and the road winds below them. |
