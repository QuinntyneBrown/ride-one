# Monaco Street Circuit

## Bends and elevation

Fixes to how the road bends and climbs here (L2-101 AC6, L2-110 AC9), from the series that began with Old Town.

They now lie along the slope, and the grandstands, barriers and props behind the rider stand on the road there instead of at the height under the rider. |
