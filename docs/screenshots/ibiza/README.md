# Ibiza Sunset Strip

## Bends and elevation

Fixes to how the road bends and climbs here (L2-101 AC6, L2-110 AC9), from the series that began with Old Town.

| Capture | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| [092 · The paint lies on the sunset strip](bend-092-paint-on-the-strip.jpg) | The words painted on the sunset strip (VAMOS, BASS, the riders' names) were laid level, so where the strip rises towards the sunset the far end of each word sank into the asphalt. They now lie along the slope, and the barriers and props behind the rider stand on the road there instead of at the height under the rider. |
