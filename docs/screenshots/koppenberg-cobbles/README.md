# Koppenberg Cobbles

## Bends and elevation

Fixes to how the road bends and climbs here (L2-101 AC6, L2-110 AC9), from the series that began with Old Town.

| Capture | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| [020 · The kit's crowd stands on the road behind](bend-020-kit-crowd-behind.jpg) | The shared crowd and mist banks of the world kit took the height under the rider for everything behind them, so on a climb like the Koppenberg's 9% the fans beside the camera floated over the road behind. They now take the road's height there, which runs back down the grade. The same fix reaches the twenty worlds built on the kit's crowd. |
| [024 · The paint lies on the Koppenberg's cobbles](bend-024-paint-on-the-cobbles.jpg) | The fans' ALLEZ and HUP HUP painted on the cobbles were laid level, so on the Koppenberg's 8–9% each word sank into the setts and was lost. They now lie along the slope, and the farm props and grazing cows behind the rider stand on the road's height there. |
