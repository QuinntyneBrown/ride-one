Foundation

Content

Short, plain, numeric. The rider is mid-effort: say the number, say what happens next.

  • Voice
  • Numbers
  • Microcopy library
  • All tokens

Voice

  • Plain words; no jargon in the UI (packets and BLE live in diagnostics).
  • Spec terms: step, cycle, resistance, target resistance.
  • Sentence case.

Numbers

  • Times m:ss, h:mm:ss over an hour.
  • Units always: W, rpm, km/h · mph, kcal.
  • Real minus for deltas: −185 W.
