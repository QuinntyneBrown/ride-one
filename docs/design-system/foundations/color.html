Foundation

Color

Wattzup is dark first: ink surfaces, chalk text and one loud colour — volt — for the thing that matters. Rest blue marks recovery, heart red marks danger and heart rate, warn amber marks waiting.

Both themes

Volt is a fill in both themes with ink text on it. In light, volt as text or edge becomes olive (--color-accent-edge, --color-fg-accent) because volt on paper is 1.1:1.
