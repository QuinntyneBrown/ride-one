Foundation

Motion

Motion explains change: a toast arriving, a level confirming, the interval bar easing between ticks. It never decorates.

  • Durations and easings

Durations and easings

Press to replay.

Rules

  • The interval bar eases over --duration-tick (linear) so 1 Hz updates look continuous.
  • Step-change banner springs in over --duration-banner.
  • Spinners use --duration-spin; skeleton shimmer --duration-shimmer.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every duration is zeroed except the spinner, which is status. The WebGL trace loop pauses when the tab is hidden and draws without easing.
