Foundation

Typography

Three families: Inter for words, Archivo condensed for numbers (tabular, so they don't jump at 1 Hz), and JetBrains Mono for raw packets. Fonts are bundled and self-hosted.

  • Families
  • Roles
  • Numeral scale
  • Measure and rules
  • All tokens

Families

  • Sans --font-family-sans: Inter, system fallback.
  • Numeral --font-family-numeral: Archivo at wdth 72, tabular figures.
  • Mono --font-family-mono: JetBrains Mono.

The mocks fall back to system stacks; the app bundles the WOFF2 files (CSP allows same-origin only).

Measure and rules

  • Body text max --layout-measure (65ch).
  • Headings never all caps except the overline role and the step-change banner.
  • Text scales to 200% without loss: sizes are rem, no fixed heights on text.
