Foundation

Responsive

Every screen works at 360px with no horizontal scroll, by touch, and gains its side panel at 1024px.

  • Rules
  • Touch
  • All tokens

Rules

  • Mobile-first min-width queries only.
  • App-bar actions become icon-only below 640px.
  • Dialogs are bottom sheets below 640px.
  • Toasts are full-width below 640px.
  • Side panels from 1024px; below, they follow main.
  • Numerals use fluid sizes so four-digit power fits at 360px.

Touch

  • Every target at least 44 × 44px (--target-comfortable) except dense rows that keep 24px with spacing (--target-min).
