Pattern

Notifications

Wattzup notifies three ways: toasts for command results, banners for connection state, and the step-change banner when the plan steps.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

See toast, alert and banner and step-change banner. There is no notification centre: the app is single-user and live.

When to use

  • Toast: result of a resistance command or a toggle.
  • Banner: Bluetooth off, simulated bike, reconnected, a browser that cannot reach Bluetooth.
  • Step-change banner: each step change, with an optional tone.

States

  • Info and success toasts dismiss after 8 s, pausing on hover and focus.
  • Warning stays until resolved; danger stays until dismissed.
  • Banners stay while the condition lasts; Reconnected clears after 4 s.

Responsive

  • Toasts bottom-right from 640px, full-width bottom below.
  • Banners full width under the app bar.

Accessibility

  • One polite live region for toasts; danger adds role="alert".
  • Notifications never take focus.
  • Auto-dismiss times are long enough to read (WCAG 2.2.1).

Content

  • Title: what happened. Text: what Wattzup is doing about it.
