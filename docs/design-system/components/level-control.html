Level control

The level control is the big − 18 + in the resistance panel. It shows the bike's reported resistance and, while a change is in flight, the target resistance.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

One press changes the target resistance by one. The large numeral is the resistance the bike reports; "Setting 22…" appears below it until the bike confirms (L2-019). The plan note under it says whether the plan or the rider is in control and offers Back to plan.

Anatomy

  1. Step buttons64px, --radius-lg, subtle fill.
  2. ResistanceNumeral 2xl (88px).
  3. Of 32Caption.
  4. TargetWarn while sending; volt when confirmed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Spread across the panel width at every breakpoint; the panel sits below the trace under 1024px.

Theming

Buttons on --color-bg-subtle; target text --color-fg-warn → --color-fg-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two buttons and a live numeral (aria-live="polite"); the target text is role="status".
