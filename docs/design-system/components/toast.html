Toast

Toasts report the result of an action: Resistance set to 20, Bike is slow to confirm, Couldn't set resistance.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Bottom-right stack, newest at the bottom, at most three. Info and success dismiss after 8 s (pausing on hover and focus); warning stays until resolved; danger never auto-dismisses.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone colour.
  2. TitleLabel.
  3. TextMuted body-sm.
  4. ActionOptional link-styled button.
  5. Close44px target.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width at the bottom below 640px (above the home indicator); 24rem bottom-right from 640px.

Theming

Raised surface; icon reads --toast-icon.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Region role="status" aria-live="polite"; danger toasts add role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction

Title
Text

Motion

Slides up over --duration-base; no motion when reduced.

Touch

Close and action are 44px / 24px targets.

Content

  • Title states the result; text gives the cause and next step.
  • Errors say what was not done: "Level 22 was not sent."
