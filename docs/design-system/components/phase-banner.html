Step-change banner

The step-change banner flashes WORK · 18 · GO! or RECOVER · 6 for 1.8 s when the plan moves to the next step, with an optional tone.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Cue only: the same information is in the interval block, and a visually hidden live region announces it ("Work, resistance 18, 40 seconds"). Never shown for the first state after load.

Anatomy

  1. TextHeavy numeral, wide tracking, glow in the phase colour.
  2. DetailOverline: round and duration.

Variants

<div class="phase-banner phase-banner--static phase-banner--rest" aria-hidden="true">RECOVER · 6<small>0:20 · next: Work at 18</small></div>

Responsive behaviour

Theming

--phase aliases --color-volt-fill or --color-series-rest.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Visual cue aria-hidden; a polite live region carries the words.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; read in document order.

Focus

Never takes focus.

Labelling

Live text: "Work, resistance 18, 40 seconds".

Contrast

Text on veil

Motion

Springs in and fades over --duration-banner; with reduced motion it appears and disappears without scaling. Tones: 990 Hz work, 520 Hz recover, 660 Hz final 3 s, only when beeps are on.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • KIND · LEVEL · GO! for effort; KIND · LEVEL for recovery.
