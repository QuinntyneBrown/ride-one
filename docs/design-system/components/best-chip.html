Personal-best chip and delta

A personal best as a volt pill, and the change since last time with its sign in the text.

Overview

The Session Summary headline carries two marks of progress: the delta, the change of average power since the previous attempt at the same class, and a personal-best chip for each best the session set (L2-087). Ended early sits in the same row.

Anatomy

  1. Change (.session-head__change): +3 W, −4 W or 0 W.
  2. Since (.session-head__since): vs last time, 17 Nov.
  3. Chip (.best-chip): trophy and the best's name.

Variants

  • Best 1-minute power
  • Best average on this class
  • No cadence dips
Two bests.

Sizes

One size.

States

Delta: --up (ack colour), --down (warn colour), --same (default). The sign is always in the text.

Responsive behaviour

Chips wrap; the delta wraps under the headline on phones.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Motion

Touch
