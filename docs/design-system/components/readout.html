Readout

Readouts are the numbers a rider reads mid-effort: Power in watts with its target and delta, and Cadence with its aim.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Hero size for power (96px, 128px from 640px), extra large for cadence (60px). Condensed tabular numerals. The sub-line compares against the plan: On target within ±8%, otherwise a signed delta.

Anatomy

  1. LabelMuted label.
  2. ValueNumeral hero.
  3. UnitCondensed, muted.
  4. Target and deltaBody-sm; delta coloured and worded.

Stale: last values at --opacity-stale while the stream or bike is down. No data: "--" while scanning or Bluetooth is off. Paused: the delta reads Paused.

Responsive behaviour

  • Power and cadence sit side by side and wrap; the interval moves below them under 1024px.

Theming

Delta: --color-fg-accent on target, --color-fg-warn below, default above.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Text. Values are not live regions: 1 Hz announcements would drown the screen reader; the rider can query them.
