Streak calendar

The streak, the last six weeks and the weekly goal.

Overview

The streak calendar is Progress's side column: the current and best streak, a message, six weeks of ridden days, this week against the goal of 4 and the all-time totals (L2-086, L2-087).

Anatomy

  1. Count (__count): the current streak.
  2. Best (__best): the best streak.
  3. Message (__message): what to do today.
  4. Grid (__grid): six weeks, Monday first.
  5. Week (__week): n of 4 rides with a meter and the totals.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section labelled Streak; the grid is a list of role="img" days named with date and state; the week is a role="meter".

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not interactive; read in reading order.

Labelling

Every day's state is in its name.

Contrast

Message (risk)
Day letters

Motion

Colour and opacity changes only, over --duration-fast; none under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

No control is smaller than 44 × 44 px.

  • Ride today to keep it alive., Today's done. See you tomorrow., One ride starts a new streak., 4 more days beats your best.
