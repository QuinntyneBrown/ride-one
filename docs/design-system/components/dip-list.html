Dip list

Every cadence dip of a ride, as a row that highlights it on the traces.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: L2-085, L2-086

Overview

The dip list names each stretch where cadence fell under its target for 6 seconds or more (L2-081), with the share of counted time on target. Each row is a button: hovering or focusing it highlights the dip on the Session traces.

Anatomy

  1. Head (__head): Cadence dips and n% on target.
  2. Lead (__lead): how many, how long in all, and last time's count.
  3. Row (__row): a button: start time, segment and round, detail, and the drop.
  4. Drop (__drop): the rpm under target, with a minus sign.

Variants

Cadence dips

With dips.

Cadence dips

99% on target

Cadence held at target for the whole ride. Nothing to fix.

No dips.

Sizes

One size.

States

Row: default, hover and focus (--color-bg-subtle, focus ring). The hovered or focused row drives the traces' focus.

Responsive behaviour

Full width of its column; from 992 px it is the 360 px side column beside the traces.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list of buttons inside a section labelled by its heading. A row does nothing on activation; it exists so keyboard users can reach the highlight.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the next dip and highlights it.
Shift+TabBack; leaving the list clears the highlight.

Labelling

Each row reads its time, segment, detail and n rpm under.

Contrast

Detail on canvas
Drop on canvas

Motion

Colour and opacity changes only, over --duration-fast; none under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

No control is smaller than 44 × 44 px.

Content

  • 3 times, 0:42 in all, your legs fell under target. Last time: 1.
  • Down to 86 rpm for 15s · target 98
  • No dips: Cadence held at target for the whole ride. Nothing to fix.
