Power trace

The power trace draws actual power as a line over the plan's target blocks, with a playhead at now.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

In the product the trace is WebGL (RideTrace) and redraws on every telemetry notification; static docs and mocks draw the same picture as SVG. Max power is max(300, peak × 1.3 rounded up to 100). Ticks every 2:00 (5:00 for long rides).

Anatomy

  1. HeadCaption title and legend.
  2. Plan blocksKind colour at 13% (7% past).
  3. Actual lineSeries actual; 12% area.
  4. PlayheadNow.
  5. AxisTime ticks, subtle.

Variants

8 × 40s / 20s at 7:07.

Legend and swatch

Legend items pair a line or swatch with a word; reused in the ride preview.

  • Recover
  • Warm-up
