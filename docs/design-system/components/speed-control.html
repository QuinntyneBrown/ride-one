Speed and incline control

The speed control and the incline control are the treadmill's − 9.0 + and − 0 + in the side panel titled Speed and incline. They replace the level control and level picker when the machine is the treadmill (L2-071).

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new

Overview

One press sends a new value at once: speed 0.5 km/h below or above the reported speed (else the target, else 7.0), incline 1 % below or above the reported incline (else the target, else 0). Two blocks with the same structure; an Angular implementation can be one component per block.

Anatomy

  1. Step buttons.step-btn, 64px, shared with the level control.
  2. Label__label, caption, names the group.
  3. ValueNumeral xl (the value the treadmill reports).
  4. Unitkm/h or %, caption.

Sizes

One size, 64px buttons.

States

VariantDefaultHoverActiveFocusDisabled
Raise speed

Both themes

Speed9.0km/h
Speed9.0km/h
  • At the limits: Lower speed is disabled at 1.0 km/h (or below, e.g. a stopped belt reporting 0.0) and Raise speed at 16.0; Lower incline at 0 % and Raise incline at 15 %.
  • Treadmill not connected: all four disabled, the plan note reads the wake-up sentence.
  • Requested vs. reported: the value here is what the treadmill reports; “Setting 9.0…” shows in the speed readout (see Readout), not here.

Responsive behaviour

  • Spread across the side panel at every width; the panel sits under the ride area below 1024px.

Theming

Buttons on --color-bg-subtle; label --color-fg-muted, unit --color-fg-subtle; no theme-specific rules.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each control is a role="group" labelled by its visible label, with two buttons and a live numeral (aria-live="polite"). Not a spinbutton: a press sends a command to the treadmill at once.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabLower speed, Raise speed, Lower incline, Raise incline in DOM order.
Enter, SpaceOne step: 0.5 km/h or 1 %.

Focus

Focus stays on the pressed button; a button that becomes disabled at its limit moves focus to its sibling.

Labelling

“Lower speed” / “Raise speed”, “Lower incline” / “Raise incline” (L2-071).

Contrast

Label on panel
Unit on panel

Motion

Press scales to 0.96 over --duration-fast; none with reduced motion beyond the colour change.

Touch

64px buttons, well over 44 × 44.

Content

  • Panel title: “Speed and incline”.
  • Values: speed with one decimal (9.0), incline as a whole number (0).
  • Wake-up note: “Speed and incline controls wake up once the treadmill connects.”
