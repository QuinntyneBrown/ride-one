App bar

The app bar carries everything about the ride that is not a number: which bike, which workout, how far in, and the Pause, End, beeps, theme and diagnostics controls.

Pause and End are disabled while there is no ride or the bike is not connected; Pause becomes Resume (play icon) when paused, with a Paused badge.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px: lockup, status and workout wrap onto their own rows; the end group takes a full row with the clock left and icon-only actions right (.btn--collapsible).
  • From 640px: one or two rows; the divider appears.
  • From 1024px: 32px side padding.

Theming

Border --color-border-default; no fill (sits on canvas).

Accessibility

Role and pattern
