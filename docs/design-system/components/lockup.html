Lockup

The lockup is the logo: a volt square with a W drawn as a rising power trace, and the wattzup wordmark.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use the standard size in the app bar (as the home link) and the large size on unlock and 404. Never recolour the mark; volt is a fill with ink stroke in both themes.

Anatomy

  1. Mark48-unit square, rx 12, 5.4 ink stroke.
  2. WordmarkNumeral family, expanded, heavy, −0.04em.
