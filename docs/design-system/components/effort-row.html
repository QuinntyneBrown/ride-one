Effort row

The effort row sets one effort of a treadmill plan: a typable speed with its pace between Slower and Faster, and the incline from 0 to 10 %.

Overview

Use an effort row for each effort of a treadmill plan in Set up your run (L2-072 AC5–7): Work and Recover for intervals, Hold for a Steady run. Each row pairs a typable speed between Slower and Faster, with its pace, and an 11-bar incline level picker from 0 to 10 %.

Responsive

Below 40rem the speed sits above the incline in one column. From 40rem the grid has two columns, speed then incline (1 : 1.15).

Theming

The rows are separated by hairlines (--color-border-default). The swatch carries the effort tone; the lit incline bars use --color-level-lit. Nothing branches on the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each row is a role="group" named by its title. The speed is a text input with a visible label; the incline is the APG Slider, named by the Incline label, with aria-valuetext Work incline 2% of 10%.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSlower, speed, Faster, then the incline slider.
← →Incline down or up by 1 %.
Page Up Page DownIncline by 2 %.
Home EndIncline 0 % or 10 %.
EnterIn the speed field, submits the form.

Labelling

Slower and Faster name the row: Slower Work, Faster Work. An error is tied to the field with aria-describedby.

Touch

Step buttons and the speed field are at least 44 × 44 px; the whole incline track is one target.

Content

Titles are the plan's words: Work, Recover, Hold. Speed in km/h with one decimal; pace as m:ss /km, or –:–– when it cannot be read; incline in whole percent.
