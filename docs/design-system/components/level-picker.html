Level picker

The level picker draws all 32 resistance levels as rising bars. Bars up to the current resistance are lit; tapping a bar requests that level.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Shows the whole range at a glance and allows a jump of many levels in one tap. The row is one slider: tapping a bar or pressing an arrow key requests that level, and aria-valuenow carries the requested level. In manual mode the plan's level gets an outline (.is-plan).

Anatomy

  1. BarHeight rises with level (--h).
  2. Lit--color-level-lit up to the level.
  3. TrackUnlit bars in --color-track.

Variants

Following the plan at 18.

Manual

Requested 22 (pressed) while the plan says 18 (outlined).

Sizes

Fills the panel width; 56px tall.

States

VariantDefaultHoverFocusDisabled
Unlit bar
Lit bar

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Bars shrink with the panel; at 360px each bar is about 8px wide. The bars are not separate targets: the whole row is one slider at least 44px tall, so a tap anywhere on it picks the level under the finger (WCAG 2.2 target size, 2.5.8).

Theming

Lit bars read --color-level-lit (volt in dark, darker volt in light for 3:1 on white).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One role="slider" named Resistance level, with aria-valuemin="1", aria-valuemax="32", aria-valuenow the requested level and aria-valuetext Level 18 of 32. The bars are decorative (aria-hidden). Until the bike connects it carries aria-disabled="true" and leaves the tab order.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
→ / ↑, ← / ↓Requests one level up or down.
Page Up / Page DownRequests four levels up or down.
Home / EndRequests level 1 / level 32.

Focus

The slider is a single tab stop and shows the focus ring around the whole row.

Labelling

"Level 22" per bar.

Contrast

Lit bar on panel

Motion

Colour over --duration-fast.

Touch

Below 1024px bars are narrow; the 64px step buttons are the touch-friendly path.

Content

  • No text; the level numeral above carries the number.

     aria-valuemax="32" aria-valuenow="18" aria-valuetext="Level 18 of 32" tabindex="0">
  <span class="level-picker__bar is-lit is-set" aria-hidden="true" style="--h: 68.5%;"></span>
</div>
