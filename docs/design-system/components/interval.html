Interval

The interval block tells the rider where they are in the plan: Work, round 5 of 8, 0:33 left, and Next: Recover 0:20 at level 6.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The time left counts down locally between 1 Hz updates. The swatch and progress colour follow the interval kind (work volt, recover blue, warm-up/cool-down grey) and the kind is always written.

Anatomy

  1. SwatchKind colour.
  2. Kind and roundLabel semibold + muted round.
  3. Time leftNumeral lg, role="timer".
  4. ProgressThin bar, kind colour.
  5. NextBody-sm muted.

Responsive behaviour

  • Beside the readouts from 1024px (max 24rem); full width below them on smaller screens.

Theming

--color-series-* for swatch and bar.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="timer" with aria-live="off": not announced each second; step changes are announced by the step-change banner's live text.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; read in document order.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

Progressbar labelled "Interval progress".

Contrast

Next text
Recover line

Motion

Countdown digits pulse (scale 1.12) at 5…1; the bar eases each tick over --duration-tick; both off under reduced motion.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • Kinds: Warm-up, Work, Recover, Cool-down, Steady.
  • Next: "Next: Recover 0:20 at level 6"; last segment: "Last stretch".
