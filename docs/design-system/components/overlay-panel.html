Component · Containers and overlays

Overlay panel

The overlay panel veils the ride area (not the whole page) when the bike state stops the ride: Lost the bike, Looking for your bike, Bluetooth is off, Ride ended; on the treadmill also the belt countdown and Belt stopped.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Unlike a dialog it is not modal: the app bar and resistance panel stay reachable, and numbers under the veil stay visible at reduced emphasis. Use it only for bike and Bluetooth states that pause the ride.

Anatomy

  1. Veil--color-bg-veil over the main column.
  2. Icon32px; heart, warn or volt.
  3. Titleh4.
  4. TextBody-sm muted.
  5. ActionOne primary.

Responsive behaviour

  • Covers only the main column; on phones that is the top part of the page and the panel stays below.

Theming

Veil 90% canvas in dark, 92% paper in light so text passes over the brightest numerals.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert" when the ride stops unexpectedly (lost, Bluetooth off); role="status" for scanning and ended.
