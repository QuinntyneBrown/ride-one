World Ride HUD

Instrument panels over a scene: the ride's live numbers and controls, plus riders nearby and a route profile.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: proposed (no requirement yet)

Overview

World Ride is the ride drawn as a place: a scene photo (or, in the product, a 3D world) with the rider's instruments on translucent panels. It shows the same live metrics and controls as the ride dashboard: estimated power against target, cadence, speed, the current segment, the route's elevation and resistance. It adds a riders-nearby board and an optional real-place scenery source.

Use it only for the World Ride screen. It is a design proposal: the mocks list the bike metrics' requirements, and a product route, scenery provider and simulated nearby riders still need a World Ride requirement (mocks README, open questions). Power is estimated (ADR 0003) and heart rate shows -- because the bike reports none.

Anatomy

  1. Bar (__bar): lockup, back to the class, the world and route (__class, __title), the bike (__bike), the ride clock (__clock, __total), Pause and End.
  2. Live metrics (__panel): estimated power (__power), target and the signed difference (__accent), then cadence, heart and speed (__stats).
  3. Riders nearby (__board, __riders, __rider--1…7): gaps to the riders ahead and behind; your row is aria-current.
  4. Scenery source (__source, __form): optional; streams a real place when the rider gives a key.
  5. Segment (__interval): kind and round, time left (__metric), a progress bar coloured by kind, and what comes next.
  6. Route profile (__profile, __route): grade, metres climbed, distance; elevation with the ridden part brighter, the plan strip and the playhead.
  7. Resistance (__resistance): − level +, as in Level control; Manual once the rider overrides the plan.

Key entry: Use key stays disabled until the field has text. Loading shows how much scenery has arrived; ready drops the panel, puts the real place in the bar and the provider's attribution at the bottom edge. Refused keeps the photographed world and offers Change key. Without WebGL the HUD runs over the canvas with no scene. Paused overlays a status panel and the world holds still; Lost the bike veils the HUD, makes it inert and offers Reconnect. Stale values follow Readout. Buttons inside the HUD keep their own states.

Responsive behaviour

Gutters scale from 12 to 24 px (--world-ride-gutter). On short landscape screens (height under 576 px) the board's rows tighten. The resistance control stays on every width: the mock hid it below 1024 px, which left phone riders unable to change resistance.

Panels use --color-bg-hud, the canvas at 0.9 opacity, so the scene shows through in either theme. Jerseys (--color-rider-*) are the same in both themes: they identify riders, not states. The route strip uses the series colours, so it turns olive and indigo on light panels.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

main is labelled with the world's name. Each panel is a section with an aria-label (Live metrics, Current segment, Route profile, Resistance); the board is an aside with a heading and an ordered list. The segment uses the WAI-ARIA progressbar; the route profile is one img with a text alternative (Route profile, 43 m of elevation). The scene photo is decorative in the product (alt=""); the mocks describe it for review.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabLockup, Class, Pause, End, the scenery field and Use key, then − and +.
Enter / SpaceActivates the focused button; Enter in the key field submits.
Tab in Lost the bikeOnly Reconnect is reachable: the HUD behind is inert.

Focus

Every control uses the system ring, which meets 3:1 on the HUD panel. Overlays do not move focus: Paused is a status, and Lost the bike puts Reconnect first in the tab order.

Labelling

Pause and Resume, and Lower and Raise resistance, have accessible names; collapsed buttons keep their label as visually hidden text. The resistance output is labelled; changes to it are not announced on every tick. Paused uses role="status", Lost the bike and a refused key use role="alert", loading scenery uses role="status".

Contrast

Ratios below are against the panel composited over the canvas. Over a photo, the worst cases are a pure white photo in dark and a pure black one in light: default text 13.9 / 14.3:1, muted 4.9 / 5.4:1, accent 13.5 / 4.65:1.

Motion

The scene moves with the rider's speed in the product; under prefers-reduced-motion it holds a still frame and only the numbers change. The animation loop pauses when the tab is hidden.

Touch

Bar buttons are 40 px tall with 44 px of row height; − and + are 48 px icon buttons. Colour is never the only carrier: riders are named, segment kinds are written, and your row is aria-current as well as accent.

Content

  • Title: the world's name (Old Town Criterium); under it, the route and the class (Cathedral Circuit · 30 Minute HIIT Cardio), or the real place once scenery is ready.
  • Power: W est., always marked as estimated; the delta is signed (-276 W) or On target.
  • Heart: -- when there is no reading; never 0.
  • Segment: Work · 1 of 8, Warm-up, Steady; Next: Recover · 3:00 at level 6.
  • Board: gaps as +m:ss ahead and −m:ss behind (true minus sign); your gap is empty.
  • Overlays: say where the rider is (Paused on Cathedral Circuit, You’re parked at 1.61 km) and what to do next.
  • Resistance: Resistance / 32 while following the plan, Manual after an override.
