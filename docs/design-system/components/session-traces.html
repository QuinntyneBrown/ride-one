Session traces

Cadence against its aim with every dip marked, and power beneath on the same clock.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: L2-085, L2-086

Overview

Session traces show how a saved ride went second by second: the cadence trace against the dashed aim of each counted segment, with a band for every cadence dip, and the power trace over shaded work segments. Both share one time axis. It is the after-the-ride cousin of the live Power trace.

Use it on the Session Summary only. A dip list beside it explains each band.

Anatomy

  1. Legend (__legend): Cadence label and the keys Actual, Target, Under target.
  2. Cadence chart (__chart): gridlines at 60, 80 and 100 rpm, dip bands, dashed aims and the cadence polyline.
  3. Power chart (__chart--power): shaded work segments and the power polyline, scaled to the ride's peak.
  4. Axis (__axis): m:ss ticks every 2, 5 or 10 minutes.

Sizes

One size: 13rem cadence and 7rem power, full column width.

States

Default: every band at 28% opacity. While a dip row is hovered or focused, the block gains --focusing: that band (__dip--focus) rises to 60% and the others fall to 12%.

Responsive behaviour

The SVGs stretch to the column (preserveAspectRatio="none", non-scaling strokes). From 992 px the traces share a row with the dip list; below, the dip list follows them.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section whose aria-label is the text alternative; the SVGs are aria-hidden. The dip list beside it carries every dip as text.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not interactive; read in reading order.

Labelling

Cadence and power over the ride: average cadence 78 rpm, 3 dips; average power 156 W, peak 453 W.

Contrast

Axis labels
Cadence line
Power line

Motion

Colour and opacity changes only, over --duration-fast; none under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

No control is smaller than 44 × 44 px.

Content

  • Labels Cadence and Power; keys Actual, Target, Under target.
  • Times as m:ss, from one hour h:mm:ss.
