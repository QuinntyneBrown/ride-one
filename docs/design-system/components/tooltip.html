Overview

Core component added for the app bar's icon buttons (the mocks show them without). The tooltip repeats the accessible name; it never holds interactive content or essential information.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerIcon button.
  2. BubbleInverse surface, caption text, 5px arrow.

Theming

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="tooltip" referenced by aria-describedby; shows on hover and on focus.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocus the trigger: tooltip shows.
EscHides it without moving focus.

Focus

Focus stays on the trigger.

Labelling

Trigger keeps its own aria-label.

Contrast

Tooltip text

Motion

Fades over --duration-fast.

Touch

Touch has no hover: the label must also be the aria-label; long-press shows the native tooltip.

Content

  • One to three words; same as the aria-label.
