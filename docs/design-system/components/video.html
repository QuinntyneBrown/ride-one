Video ride-along

A YouTube video played in step with the ride: picked and lined up on the setup screen, shown in place of the power trace on the dashboard.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: proposed (no requirement yet)

Overview

Proposed in the 2026-11-17 Magic Patterns frames. It needs a requirement and an ADR for the CSP frame-src before it is built (mocks README, open question 14).

Anatomy

  1. Video frame (.video-frame): 16:9 on the setup screen; it fills the trace area on the dashboard (--fill).
  2. Start point (.video-sync): a number stepper in half seconds, Use video's current time and Check.
  3. View switch (.view-switch): tabs between Video and Power vs. plan.
  4. Plan strip (.profile--strip): the plan under the video, with the playhead.

States

  • Playing / paused: follows the ride; nothing on the frame changes.
  • Blocked: the browser refused autoplay; a scrim with Play video in sync covers the frame.
  • Invalid link: the link field shows its field__error; no frame or start point.
Blocked.
HTML
<div class="video-frame"><button class="video-frame__scrim" type="button"><span class="btn btn--primary btn--lg">Play video in sync</span></button></div>

Responsive behaviour

  • The start-point row wraps: the actions drop under the stepper below about 30rem.
  • On the dashboard under 1024 px the frame keeps 16:9.

Theming

The frame is dark in both themes: --color-video-bg, --color-video-fg, --color-video-scrim. The tab switch uses --color-bg-subtle with the selected tab on --color-bg-surface-raised.

Accessibility

The switch is a tablist with roving tabindex; arrow keys move between tabs. The scrim is a real button. The player's own controls stay keyboard reachable inside the iframe, whose title is the video title. Every target is at least 44 × 44 px.

Content

  • Toggle: Ride along to a video · Plays in sync on the dashboard.
  • Start point: Ride 0:00 starts at, written m:ss.s.
  • Preview row: Video · Synced from 0:12.0.
  • Error: That doesn't look like a YouTube link. Paste a youtube.com or youtu.be link.
  • Blocked: Play video in sync.

Do and don't

  • Do let the ride clock lead; the video follows.
  • Don't hold the ride for a buffering video.

Tokens

--color-video-*, --radius-lg, --target-comfortable, --space-0-5 switch padding.
