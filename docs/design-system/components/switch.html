Switch

A switch turns a setting on or off immediately in the form: Warm-up (3 min, easy) and Cool-down (2 min, easy).

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use for a setting with two states that applies at once to the plan preview. In setup, switches sit in .toggle-rows: label left, switch right, full-row hit area.

Anatomy

  1. Track44 × 24px, volt when on (olive edge in light).
  2. Knob20px, surface colour.
  3. LabelNames the setting.

Variants

Toggle rows in setup.

<span class="toggle-row__label">

</span>

<input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked>
</span>

Responsive behaviour

  • Toggle rows are full width at every breakpoint; the row is the 44px target.

Theming

Track on: --color-accent; off: --color-border-strong; knob: --color-bg-surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Checkbox with role="switch" (APG Switch).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
SpaceToggles.

Focus

Ring around the track.

Labelling

The row label names the setting ("Warm-up"), not the action.

Contrast

Off track on canvas
On track edge on canvas

Motion

Knob slides over --duration-base; instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Whole toggle row is the target.

Content

  • Label is the setting; the small text says what on means: "3 min, easy".
